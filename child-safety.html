Child safety standards

4DS (4DASISTAS) · Last updated October 29, 2026

4DS is a community app for adult Muslim women in the Greater Toronto Area. It is made for adults (18+) and is not intended for children. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and for child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Our standards

  • Sexual content of any kind, and any content that sexualises or endangers a child, is forbidden. Nothing like it may be posted, shared or linked in 4DS.
  • 4DS has no public chat or direct messaging between strangers. Members only see names, club memberships and the events and ideas shared inside their clubs.
  • Photos and event pages are added by the 4DS admin team. Members can only upload a profile picture and their own answers to event forms.
  • If we learn that someone under 13 has an account, we delete it.

How to report a concern

Email admin@4dasistas.ca, or message us on Instagram @4dasistas.ca. We read every report.

What we do when we are told

  • We remove the content and the account straight away.
  • We report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation to the proper authorities, including the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (Cybertip.ca) and law enforcement, and we cooperate with their investigations.

Designated contact

The person responsible for child safety and CSAM prevention at 4DS can be reached at admin@4dasistas.ca.
