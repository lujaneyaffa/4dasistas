Privacy Policy

4DS (4DASISTAS) · Last updated October 21, 2026

4DS is a community website and app for Muslim women in the GTA: events, clubs, an availability calendar, a badminton leaderboard and a steps challenge. This page explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, a username, and a 4-digit PIN (stored scrambled, never in plain text). Optionally a phone / WhatsApp number and a profile picture.
  • What you do in the community: the clubs you belong to, your availability, event RSVPs, answers to event sign-up forms (and a map pin if a form asks for a location), votes and ideas, badminton games, attendance and points.
  • Steps (only if you turn it on, in the app): your daily step total from Apple Health or Health Connect, for the steps challenge. We read step counts only — nothing else from Health — and never use it for advertising.
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send you notifications you allowed.
  • Basic technical data: like most websites, our host records standard request information (such as IP address) for security and to prevent abuse.

How we use it

To run the community: show you your clubs and events, send reminders and updates you've asked for, organise availability, run the leaderboards and the steps challenge, and let organizers contact you (for example on WhatsApp) about clubs and events you joined. We do not show ads and we do not sell your data.

Who can see what

  • Organizers (admins) can see members' names, usernames, phone numbers, clubs and sign-up answers so they can run clubs and events.
  • Other members can see your name and the clubs, events and leaderboards you take part in, as the app shows.
  • The public can see the badminton and steps leaderboards only as a first name with a last initial (for example “Aya T.”) — never phone numbers, usernames or photos.

Who processes data for us

We use Cloudflare (hosting and storage), Resend (email), Apple and Google (push notifications), and Google Maps / OpenStreetMap (maps and place search). They handle data only to provide those services.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete your account yourself any time in the app: Settings → Delete my account. That removes your profile, availability, RSVPs, step totals and club memberships. Badminton games you played stay as an anonymous “Former player” so other people's scores don't change. You can also ask us to delete or correct your data through the contact below.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS and PINs are stored scrambled. No system is perfect, so please pick a PIN you don't use elsewhere.

Children

4DS is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: message us on Instagram @4dasistas.ca or see Help & contact.
