4DS (4DASISTAS)

Need help or have a question?

Message us on Instagram: @4dasistas.ca. We reply as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • I forgot my PIN. Message us and an admin will reset it for you.
  • How do I log in? With your username or your phone number, plus your 4-digit PIN.
  • How do I join a club? Open the Clubs tab and tap “Request to join”. An admin approves each request.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app: Settings → Delete my account. It removes your profile and everything linked to it.
  • Where is the privacy policy? Privacy policy.
