Delete your 4DS account

4DS (4DASISTAS) · the community app for Muslim women in the GTA

In the app or on the website (fastest)

  1. Open the 4DS app or go to 4dasistas.ca and log in.
  2. Tap Profile (the person icon).
  3. Go to Settings → Delete my account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Can't log in?

Message us on Instagram @4dasistas.ca from the account you signed up with (or tell us your username) and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it for you.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, username, PIN, phone number, profile picture)
  • Your availability, event RSVPs and sign-up answers
  • Your club memberships and join requests
  • Your step totals and any steps-challenge entries
  • Your notification (push) token

What is kept

Badminton games you played stay in the leaderboards as an anonymous “Former player” so other people's scores don't change. Nothing that identifies you is kept. Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

See also our Privacy Policy.
